# Riffle Privacy Policy

_Effective 6 October 2026_

This policy covers Riffle on every platform (Windows, including the Microsoft Store version, Android and Linux).
Riffle does not collect, store or share any personal information.

## What stays on your device

Riffle saves your flashcard decks, settings, study statistics, quiz history and card edits only on your own
device. It has no accounts or sign-in, and it never uploads your data anywhere. If you export a backup or your
statistics, that file goes where you save it and nowhere else.

## No tracking

Riffle contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.

## Internet use

Riffle connects to the internet only in these cases:

- **Deck content.** A deck you open may load its own web content, such as fonts, images or maths libraries. Riffle
  keeps a copy so the deck also works offline. Decks cannot reach your local network or other devices on it.
- **Links.** A link you click inside a deck opens in your web browser.
- **Update checks.** Copies downloaded from GitHub check
  [Riffle's GitHub releases page](https://github.com/Naved124/Riffle/releases) for new versions, and download
  an update only after you agree to install it. You can turn the check off in **Settings → About**. The
  Microsoft Store version gets its updates from the Microsoft Store and never makes this check.

These are ordinary web requests. As with any website you visit, the server contacted (for example, GitHub or a
font provider) can see your IP address. Riffle sends no personal information with these requests.

## Children

Riffle does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page, with a new effective date.
